# Hotel bookings module

Hotel reservations (`hotel_bookings`). The rooms and their nights belong to the hotels module, and this module reaches them only through `HotelRoomHoldsService` (see "Holding rooms for a reservation" in the hotels README).

## Reserve rooms and hold them for payment (HTL-16, HTL-18)

- `POST /api/hotel-bookings` is for registered users (role `user`) only. Guests get 401, administrators and moderators get 403.
- The body is the hotel, the dates, and the rooms with the guests of each: `adults` and `childAges` (each 0 to 17). Every room needs at least one guest, and the reservation needs at least one adult in total (HTL-16), so a room with only children is allowed. Each room can be chosen once, and a reservation has 1 to 20 rooms, the same limit as the search (HTL-11). Room ids are compared in lower case.
- The client never sends a price, status, owner, hold or reference. `forbidNonWhitelisted` answers 400 for them.
- The checks run in this order, cheapest first: the body (400), the stay (400: check-in not in the past, at most 366 nights, the same as search), the hotel is active (404), every room is a room of this hotel (404), no room has more guests than its maximum occupancy (400), every room is free for the whole stay (409, nothing is held). Then the rooms are held (409 if someone else got a night first).
- Going to payment is this call, so it is when the 10-minute hold of HTL-18 starts. A room is refused (409, nothing held) when any night of the stay is booked by a Confirmed reservation, held by a hold that has not ended, or blocked by the moderator; the check-out day is not a night of the stay, so back-to-back stays are allowed. The reservation is created as `pending_payment` with `holdExpiresAt` = now + 10 minutes, and every night of every room is held in `room_nights` with the same moment.
- All or nothing: the rooms are held first and the reservation is saved after. If saving the reservation fails, the nights it held are released. There is no transaction, because the CI database is a single MongoDB server; the unique (`roomId`, `date`) index of `room_nights` decides who gets a night, and the e2e test sends five requests for the same room at the same moment (one gets 201, four get 409).
- The total is the sum over the rooms of nights x the room's price at that moment, in whole minor units of the platform currency. Each booked room keeps its own price, a label (`101 (Double)`) and its guests, so later changes to the room do not change the reservation (HTL-05).
- The hotel's check-in and check-out times and its cancellation tiers are copied into the reservation for the confirmation (HTL-17) and the cancellation (HTL-21). A hotel that never set its policies can still be booked: the times are then `null` and the tiers empty, which means no refund. The tiers are stored and returned with the highest threshold first.
- `reference` stays empty until the payment succeeds and the reservation is confirmed (HTL-17), so its unique index is `sparse`.

## Hold expiry (HTL-18, GEN-11)

If the payment does not succeed within the 10 minutes, the reservation becomes `expired` and its rooms are released (GEN-11: Pending Payment -> Expired).

- **A job, every minute.** `HotelBookingExpiryService.expireDueHoldsJob` runs with `@Cron(EVERY_MINUTE)`, through the `ScheduleModule.forRoot()` that `AppModule` already registers for flights (FLT-22). It reads the `pending_payment` reservations whose `holdExpiresAt` is at or before now, oldest first, at most `HOTEL_BOOKING_EXPIRY_BATCH_SIZE` (100) per run, with the (`status`, `holdExpiresAt`) index of the spec. A larger backlog is cleared in a few runs.
- **Expire first, then release.** For each reservation, an update that matches only `{ _id, status: pending_payment, holdExpiresAt <= now }` sets `expired`. Only when it matched does the job call `HotelRoomHoldsService.release(bookingId)`, which deletes the `held` nights of that reservation and never touches booked or blocked nights. So a reservation confirmed in between (HTL-17) is never released, and one that two runs see at the same moment is released once. The confirmation (HTL-17) uses the mirror condition (`holdExpiresAt > now`), so a reservation can never be both confirmed and released.
- **Never a crash.** One failing reservation is logged and the others go on; the cron method catches every error. A release that fails after the status changed is logged: the nights keep their `holdExpiresAt`, which has passed, so every query of the hotels module already ignores them, and the next hold of those nights removes them.
- **Between the end of the hold and the next run** (at most one minute) the reservation still reads `pending_payment`, but its rooms are already free: the hotels module ignores a held night whose `holdExpiresAt` has passed. An expired reservation is never revived; a user who still wants the rooms makes a new reservation.
- **Tests:** `hotel-booking-expiry.service.spec.ts` (the exact query, sort, limit, update and release; a reservation confirmed in between; two runs at once; a failed update and a failed release; the cron metadata and the job's error handling) and the "expiry of unpaid holds" block of `test/hotel-bookings.e2e-spec.ts` (a two-room reservation expires and both rooms are booked again by someone else, a second run changes nothing, a confirmed reservation keeps its booked nights, a hold that has not ended is untouched, the job method itself).

## Confirmation after payment (HTL-17, PAY-01)

- **Who calls it.** The payments webhook (PAY-01), once Stripe reports that the money arrived and the amount matches. `HotelBookingsService.confirmPaid(bookingId, now)` is the `BookingPaymentHandler` that `PaymentsModule` finds on this service; it delegates to `HotelBookingConfirmationService`. No route confirms a reservation: the browser never decides.
- **Order: keep the nights, confirm, book the nights, notify.**
  1. **Keep the nights** (`HotelRoomHoldsService.secureHold`): the reservation's held nights whose hold is still live at `now` get a hold end 30 minutes later (`CONFIRM_NIGHTS_GRACE_MINUTES`). Only that reservation's nights, found by `bookingId`, are changed. The reservation is read first: only when **every** night (rooms x nights) was kept does the confirmation go on. The webhook's `now` is taken a moment before this step, so a night whose hold has just ended may already have been taken by another reservation's hold; then the payment is late and is not confirmed.
  2. **Confirm** with one `findOneAndUpdate` that matches `{ _id, status: pending_payment, holdExpiresAt: { $gt: now } }` and sets `status: confirmed` and the `reference`. The expiry job matches `holdExpiresAt <= now`, so the two can never both win, and the job releases nights only when its own update matched: it never releases a confirmed reservation (tested end to end).
  3. **Book the nights** (`HotelRoomHoldsService.confirm`): every `held` night of this reservation becomes `booked` and loses its `holdExpiresAt`, all rooms in one `updateMany` by `bookingId`. The number changed must be rooms x nights; otherwise an error with the reservation id and both numbers is logged.
  4. **Notify** the owner (GEN-01, `booking_confirmed`, linked to the `hotel_booking`).
- **Why step 1.** Unlike flight seats, a held night stops counting the moment its hold ends, and the next reservation's hold deletes expired held nights before it takes them. Without step 1, a payment confirmed in the last moments of the hold could see its nights taken between steps 2 and 3, and the room would be double booked. With step 1 the nights stay held for 30 more minutes, so steps 2 and 3 always find them. If the reservation then cannot be confirmed (the payment is late, or it is no longer pending), its held nights are released at once, so nothing stays blocked.
- **Why confirm before booking the nights.** The confirmation is the user's proof of payment, so it is saved first and never undone. If booking the nights fails with a database error, the error is thrown: the reservation stays confirmed, the webhook answers 500, and Stripe's retry books the nights while they are still held (30 minutes). A crash between steps 2 and 3 is repaired the same way. If the update changes fewer than rooms x nights, an error with the reservation id and both numbers is logged.
- **Idempotent.** When the update does not match, the reservation is read: one with a reference was confirmed before (it keeps the reference when it is completed or cancelled later), so the answer is `true`, any night still held is booked, and no second notification is sent.
- **A late payment is not confirmed.** A reservation whose hold had ended (or is expired, or does not exist) answers `false` without throwing, so Stripe gets 200 and the payments module logs it for the refund (PAY-03). A database error throws, so the webhook answers 500 and Stripe retries.
- **The reference** looks like `HB-7KQ2MX`: `HB-` (hotel booking, never mixed up with a flight `FB-`) and 6 characters from `23456789ABCDEFGHJKMNPQRSTUVWXYZ`, without the look-alikes 0/O and 1/I/L, drawn with `crypto.randomInt` (about 887 million values). The unique sparse index on `reference` decides uniqueness: on a duplicate key the confirming update is tried again with a new reference, at most 5 times (`REFERENCE_ATTEMPTS`).
- **The notification** names the reference, the hotel, the check-in and check-out dates exactly as stored (`YYYY-MM-DD` strings, never converted to a `Date`, so no time zone can shift them), the nights, each room and the guests, for example "Your reservation HB-7KQ2MX at Nile Hotel is confirmed: check-in 2027-06-15, check-out 2027-06-18 (3 nights), 2 rooms (101 (Double), 102 (Family)), 3 adults and 1 child." If reading the hotel or sending fails, it is logged with the reservation id only and the reservation stays confirmed.
- **For the auth module (ACC-12):** `hasConfirmedBooking(userId)` and `hasLiveHold(userId, now)`, read only: whether the user has a `confirmed` reservation, or a `pending_payment` one whose `holdExpiresAt` is after `now`. `AccountDeactivationService` refuses to deactivate the account while either is true. Each is one count with a limit of 1 (not `exists()`, which stalled in local e2e runs against the Atlas test database). There is no index on `userId`: a user has few reservations and the route is rare.
- **Confirmation page.** The page reads the reservation with `GET /api/hotel-bookings/:id` (HTL-20, below), which returns the `reference` and the `status`.
- **For the admin module (ACC-15):** `findTravellerIdsOfHotel(hotelId, now)`, read only: the distinct user ids with a `confirmed` reservation of the hotel whose `checkOut` is today or later (UTC date), used to notify the travellers when the hotel is deactivated.

## Booking lifecycle (GEN-11)

GEN-11: "Flight bookings and hotel reservations shall follow the lifecycle Pending Payment -> Confirmed -> Completed, with Pending Payment -> Expired (hold timeout) and Confirmed -> Cancelled (user or provider cancellation) as the only other transitions."

- **One table.** `hotel-booking-lifecycle.ts` holds the only transitions (`HOTEL_BOOKING_TRANSITIONS`, `canHotelBookingMoveTo`): `pending_payment` -> `confirmed` or `expired`, `confirmed` -> `completed` or `cancelled`; `completed`, `expired` and `cancelled` are final. Every status change is a conditional update that matches the status it leaves, so two jobs or a job and a request at the same moment can never make a transition outside the table.
- **Who does each transition.** Pending Payment -> Confirmed: the confirmation after payment (PAY-01 and HTL-17). Pending Payment -> Expired: the hold-expiry job (HTL-18). Confirmed -> Completed: the completion job below. Confirmed -> Cancelled: the cancellation (HTL-21, below), which matches `status: confirmed` the same way.
- **Completion job.** `HotelBookingCompletionService` runs every hour (`@Cron(EVERY_HOUR)`, the `ScheduleModule` of `AppModule`) and catches and logs every error. A confirmed reservation is completed from the day after its check-out date in UTC (`checkOut < today`): the hotel's local check-out time is not known in UTC, and a day later every guest has left, so a guest still at the hotel is never completed. One `updateMany` with `{ status: confirmed, checkOut < today }` on the new (`status`, `checkOut`) index completes them. The update matches only a reservation still `confirmed`, so a cancellation at the same moment wins and is never overwritten. Up to one hour late, which only delays the label.
- **Tests:** `hotel-booking-lifecycle.spec.ts`, `hotel-booking-completion.service.spec.ts` (the exact filter at 00:30 UTC and at the end of a year, the cron and its error handling), the schema index test and `hotel-bookings.module.spec.ts`.

## Cancel a reservation (HTL-21)

HTL-21: "The system shall allow a registered user to cancel their own Confirmed reservation as a whole before the check-in date, after viewing the refund computed from the hotel's tiers (HTL-10) on the full total. The reservation becomes Cancelled, all its rooms are released for those dates and a refund is created (PAY-03). Partial cancellation is not supported."

| Route                                 | Access                                        |
| ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| `GET /api/hotel-bookings/:id/cancel`  | Registered user (role `user`), own only (404) |
| `POST /api/hotel-bookings/:id/cancel` | Registered user (role `user`), own only (404) |

It mirrors the flight cancellation (FLT-26).

- **View, then confirm.** `GET .../cancel` shows the refund and changes nothing. `POST .../cancel` takes `{ expectedRefundPercent }`, the percent the user was shown; if the refund is not that any more (a tier boundary passed between the two calls), the answer is 409 "The refund changed since you viewed it…" and nothing changes. The client never sends an amount.
- **Who and when.** Only the owner (another user's reservation is the same 404 as a missing one, ACC-11), only a `confirmed` reservation (`canHotelBookingMoveTo(status, cancelled)`, GEN-11; 409 "Only a confirmed reservation can be cancelled" otherwise), the whole reservation only, and only **before the check-in date**: the check-in date is compared with today's UTC date, because the hotel's time zone is not stored. On the check-in day itself and after it the answer is 409 "The check-in date has been reached…".
- **The refund.** `daysRemaining` is the whole number of days from today to the check-in date (both UTC dates; 1 the day before check-in). `refundPercent` is `refundPercentFor(cancellationTiers, daysRemaining)` (`common/cancellation-tiers.ts`) with the tiers **copied into the reservation** when it was made (HTL-10), so a later policy change never changes it. The amount is `round(total × refundPercent / 100)` on the full total of every room, in USD cents.
- **One conditional update.** `{ _id, userId, status: confirmed }` sets `status: cancelled`, `cancelledAt`, `cancelledBy: user` and `refundPercent` (the fields of the data model). `matchedCount` 0 means another cancellation or the completion job won in between: 409, and nothing else happens. So two cancellations at the same moment cancel once (tested end to end).
- **Then, never undoing the cancellation:** `HotelRoomHoldsService.unbook(bookingId)` deletes every night of every room of the reservation, booked or still held (a small method added to the hotels module; a night still held is possible when the cancellation comes between the confirmation and the booking of its nights, and deleting it means a later retry of the confirmation finds nothing to book); if it frees fewer than rooms x nights, or fails, it is logged with the reservation id. Then `RefundsService.refund({ bookingType: hotel, bookingId, refundPercent, reason: user_cancellation })` creates the refund through Stripe; a 0% refund creates nothing (`refund: null`), and a failure is logged and the reservation stays cancelled: a refund that was recorded but not accepted by Stripe yet stays `pending` and is resent by the PAY-03 recovery job, while a refund that could not even be recorded is only in the log (as in FLT-26). The refund sends its own `refund` notification (PAY-03).
- **`CancelledBy`** is the enum of the flight bookings (`flight-bookings/enums/cancelled-by.enum.ts`, values `user` and `provider`, the data model's "user or provider"). It is a plain enum file with no import, so using it creates no module cycle, and both kinds of booking store the same values.
- **Where it lives:** `HotelBookingCancellationController` and `HotelBookingCancellationService`, new files next to the booking routes.
- **Tests:** `hotel-booking-cancellation.service.spec.ts` (`daysUntil` over a month, a year and a leap day; every tier, no tiers, rounding; 404; 409 for every other status and on and after the check-in day; the exact conditional update; a changed refund, a lost race, fewer nights freed, a failed release, a failed refund and a 0% refund), `dto/cancel-hotel-booking.dto.spec.ts`, `hotel-booking-cancellation.controller.spec.ts`, the schema tests of the new fields, `HotelRoomHoldsService.unbook` in the hotels module, and `test/hotel-booking-cancellation.e2e-spec.ts` (on the `-test` database, through the real reservation, payment and webhook, with `createRefund` replaced in every test: the preview changes nothing; a two-room reservation is cancelled, every night is free again and another user books the same rooms, one refund of the right amount with its idempotency key; a lower tier; a wrong percent; two cancellations at once; already cancelled; waiting for payment; on the check-in day; another user and a missing reservation; 401, 403, 400; Swagger).

## My hotel reservations (HTL-20)

HTL-20: "The system shall allow a registered user to view their hotel reservations grouped as Upcoming, Past and Cancelled, showing reference, hotel, rooms, dates, guests, total and status, and to open full details."

| Route                         | Access                                        |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| `GET /api/hotel-bookings`     | Registered user (role `user`), own only       |
| `GET /api/hotel-bookings/:id` | Registered user (role `user`), own only (404) |

- **The grouping rule** (`hotel-reservation-groups.ts`, one place for the list filter and the label of each item, so they can never disagree; FLT-25 can mirror it for flights). "Today" is the UTC date, as for the check-in rule of HTL-16, because the hotel's time zone is not stored.
  - **Cancelled:** status `cancelled`.
  - **Past:** status `completed`, or `confirmed` with a check-out before today. The completion job (GEN-11) runs every hour, so a confirmed reservation whose check-out has passed is already Past before the job labels it `completed`.
  - **Upcoming:** status `confirmed` with a check-out today or later, so a stay that is going on is Upcoming until its last day.
  - **In no group:** `pending_payment` and `expired`. A reservation waiting for payment is paid from the reservation itself, and an expired hold was never a stay. The details route still opens them (`group: null`), so the payment page can read a pending reservation.
- **The list** (`GET /api/hotel-bookings`) filters by the logged-in user and, with `group`, by one group. Without `group` the three groups are listed together. Order: Upcoming soonest check-in first; Past most recent check-out first; Cancelled and the whole list most recent check-in first; `_id` breaks ties so pages never repeat or skip. Paginated (`page`, `limit`, 10 by default), with `paginate()`. Each item: id, reference, hotel (id and name), rooms (label, adults, children's ages), check-in, check-out, nights, guests (adults and children over all rooms), total, currency, status and group. The hotel names of a page are read in one query (`HotelsService.findNamesByIds`, a small read-only method added to the hotels module); a hotel that no longer exists shows `name: null`.
- **The details** (`GET /api/hotel-bookings/:id`): everything the confirmation page and the details need, including the hotel times, each room's price and subtotal, the cancellation tiers copied at booking time and the refund with its status (PAY-03, `RefundsService.findSummaryByBooking`, `null` when there is none). Another user's reservation and a missing one are the same 404 "Booking not found" (ACC-11); a bad id is 400.
- **Nothing internal leaves the API:** `holdExpiresAt`, `policyAcceptedAt`, the owner id and the Mongo fields are never returned (new mappers in `mappers/hotel-reservation.mapper.ts`). The response of `POST /api/hotel-bookings` keeps `holdExpiresAt`, because the payment countdown needs it.
- **Index:** (`userId`, `status`, `checkIn`) for the list.
- **Where it lives:** `HotelReservationsController` and `HotelReservationsService`, new files next to the booking routes, so HTL-21 to HTL-23 can add their own without touching these.
- **Tests:** `hotel-reservation-groups.spec.ts` (every status on both sides of today, and the list filter agrees with the label for every case), `dto/hotel-reservations-query.dto.spec.ts`, `mappers/hotel-reservation.mapper.spec.ts`, `hotel-reservations.service.spec.ts`, `hotel-reservations.controller.spec.ts`, the schema index test, `HotelsService.findNamesByIds` in the hotels module, and `test/hotel-reservations.e2e-spec.ts` (on the `-test` database, reservations inserted in every status: each group, the whole list and its order, pages with no repeat, nothing internal, the details with a refund, a pending reservation, 404 for another user, 400, 401, 403, Swagger).

## Hotel ratings and reviews (HTL-23)

HTL-23: "A Confirmed reservation becomes Completed once its check-out date has passed. The system shall allow the user to submit one rating (1-5) and an optional review (max 1000 characters) per Completed reservation, regardless of the number of rooms, and later edit or delete it. The hotel's average rating is recalculated accordingly."

| Route                                 | Access                                              |
| ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| `POST /api/hotel-bookings/:id/review` | Registered user (role `user`), own reservation only |
| `PATCH /api/reviews/:id`              | The review's author only (404 for others)           |
| `DELETE /api/reviews/:id`             | The review's author only (404 for others)           |

- **Reviews belong to this module** (the module table: hotel-bookings owns "hotel reviews" and `/api/reviews`). `Review` follows `REVIEW` of the data model: collection `reviews`, `hotelId`, `userId`, `bookingId` (unique), `rating` (a whole number 1 to 5), an optional `text` (trimmed, at most 1000 characters), `moderation` (`visible` or `hidden`, `visible` by default) and the timestamps. Indexes: unique `bookingId`; (`hotelId`, `createdAt` descending) for the hotel's reviews.
- **Only a completed stay** (`status: completed`, which the completion job sets the day after check-out, GEN-11) of the user's own reservation: another user's or a missing reservation is the same 404 "Booking not found" (ACC-11); any other status is 409 "Only a completed stay can be reviewed…".
- **One per reservation, whatever the number of rooms.** A second review is 409 "This reservation already has a review…": checked first, and the unique `bookingId` index decides two reviews at the same moment. After a delete, the stay can be reviewed again.
- **Edit and delete.** `PATCH /api/reviews/:id` changes the rating, the text, or both (at least one, 400 otherwise); an empty text removes it; a sent `null` is 400 (`UpdateReviewDto` uses `whenSent` instead of `PartialType`, as `UpdateCabinDto` does, because `PartialType` adds `@IsOptional()`, which lets `null` through). `DELETE` answers 204. Both look the review up by id **and** author, so another user's review is a 404, never a 403.
- **The hotel's rating is recomputed, never added to.** After every create, edit and delete, one aggregation over the hotel's **visible** reviews gives the count and the average (rounded to two decimals, 0 and 0 when there is none), and `HotelsService.setRating` stores both (a small method added to the hotels module). Two changes at the same moment could each store what they read before the other's write, so after storing, the rating is read again and stored again if it moved (at most 3 times): the last writer always stores what every change left. If storing fails (or the process stops before it), it is logged and the review stays saved; the rating is then stale until the next change to that hotel's reviews, which recomputes everything from scratch. If the rating keeps moving for 3 tries, the newest value read is stored once more.
- **The response** has the id, the hotel and reservation ids, the rating, the text (`null` when there is none), the hotel's `reply` (`null` when there is none, HTL-26) and the times. Never the author's id, the moderation flag or the id of the moderator who replied.
- **Not here:** the public list of a hotel's reviews with the "Deleted user" author display (HTL-24, ACC-12) is `GET /api/hotels/:id/reviews` in the hotels module, which reads this collection through `HotelReviewLookup`. Reporting a review (GEN-09) is in "Not in this module yet"; the moderator's reply is in "Replies to reviews (HTL-26)".
- **Tests:** `models/review.schema.spec.ts`, `dto/review.dto.spec.ts` (both DTOs through the global pipe: the rating bounds and whole numbers, the text limit and trimming, `null`, server fields), `mappers/review.mapper.spec.ts`, `reviews.service.spec.ts` (owner and status checks, the existing review and the unique-index race, a database error, the edit with `$set` or `$unset`, an empty edit, 404s, the aggregation of visible reviews and the rounding, no review left, a rating moved in between and stored again, the limit, a failed store), `reviews.controller.spec.ts`, `HotelsService.setRating` in the hotels module, and `test/hotel-reviews.e2e-spec.ts` (on the `-test` database, completed reservations inserted with the model: create, the hotel's count and average after create, edit and delete, a new review after a delete, a duplicate, a confirmed and a cancelled reservation, another user's reservation, every 400, another user's edit and delete, 401, 403, Swagger).

## Replies to reviews (HTL-26)

HTL-26 (optional, approved by the team leader): "The system shall allow a hotel moderator to reply to a review."

| Route                         | Access                                                         |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `POST /api/reviews/:id/reply` | Hotel moderator (role `hotel_moderator`) of the review's hotel |

- **The reply is a field of the review**, as the data model says ("hotel replies to reviews, a field on the review"): an optional sub-document `reply` with `text`, `repliedAt` and `moderatorId` (`ReviewReply`, `_id: false`, every field with an explicit type). No new collection and no new index. `moderatorId` records who replied and never leaves the API.
- **The text** is trimmed and has 1 to 1000 characters (`REPLY_TEXT_MAX_LENGTH`). The workbook gives no limit for a reply, so it has the limit of a review's text. `CreateReviewReplyDto` has only `text`; `repliedAt`, `moderatorId` and any other field are refused (400) by the global pipe, and the server sets them.
- **Who may reply.** Only the role `hotel_moderator` (403 for any other role, 401 without a login). The moderator's hotel is checked with `HotelsService.findManagedEntityOrThrow`, the same check as HTL-22: the hotel is the review's and the moderator's `assignment` is read from their account, never from the request. Another hotel's moderator gets the same 403 "You do not manage this hotel" as in HTL-22.
- **Which review.** A missing review and a hidden one (GEN-10) are the same 404 "Review not found", so a hidden review is never revealed.
- **One reply per review.** A second reply is 409 "This review already has a reply…" and changes nothing. The first read only chooses between 404, 403 and 409. The reply is stored by **one** conditional `updateOne` (the review id, `moderation: visible`, no `reply` yet) and its `matchedCount` is checked: of two replies at the same moment exactly one matches, and the other is 409 (or 404 if the review was hidden or deleted in between).
- **What a reply does not change.** The hotel's `ratingCount` and `averageRating` (no rating is recomputed) and the review's `updatedAt`, which stays the author's last change (`timestamps: false` on the update; `repliedAt` is the reply's time). No notification is created.
- **The author's routes.** `PATCH /api/reviews/:id` changes only the rating and the text, so the reply stays. `DELETE /api/reviews/:id` deletes the review with its reply.
- **The response** is 201 with the review (`ReviewResponseDto`) and its `reply: { text, repliedAt }` (`ReviewReplyResponseDto`). The HTL-23 routes return `reply` too (`null` when there is none). The public list of a hotel's reviews (HTL-24, hotels module) shows the reply as `reply` when there is one; it is the only change outside this module.
- **Not built, on purpose:** editing or deleting a reply. Section 8 of the project description has no route for it, so it would be a follow-up issue (for example `PATCH` and `DELETE /api/reviews/:id/reply`).
